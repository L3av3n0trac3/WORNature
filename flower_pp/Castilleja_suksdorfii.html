Castilleja suksdorfii

Common name
Suksdorf's Paintbrush
Family
Orobanchaceae
Family common name
Broomrape Family
Blooms
June - September
Habitat
Wet meadows in upper montane to subalpine areas.
Inflorescence color
Mixture of red, yellow and green.
Stature
12-25", erect.
Leaves
Upper leaves divided into 3-=5 lobes; lower leaves linear, entire.
Florets
Flower bracts and calyx bright red at top, pale green on lower section often separated by a narrow yellow band, narrowly-lobed, mostly 5 lobes with sharply pointed tips.
