Symphyotrichum subspicatum

was

Aster subspicatus

Common name
Douglas' Aster
Family
Asteraceae
Family common name
Sunflowers, Daisies, Asters, and Allies Family
Blooms
June - October
Habitat
On beaches, along streams, disturbed areas, other moist sites, at low elevations.
Recognition
Cluster of erect leafy stems from spreading roots. Stems with many hairy leaves. Leaves toothed; lower leaves lance-shaped,with short petiole. Midstem leaves usually without petiole but do not clasp stem. Cup of overlapping bracts, the outer bracts with paper-like margins and light yellow to reddish-brown base; ray flowers purple to blue, about 1/2 to 1 inch long.
