Castilleja applegatei

Common name
Wavyleaf Paintbrush
Family
Orobanchaceae
Family common name
Broomrape Family
Blooms
April - August
Habitat
Sagebrush, open conifer woods.
Inflorescence color
Red-orange, but can be yellowish.
Stature
12-25", erect.
Leaves
Green, wavy along edges, undivided below, divided into 3 lobes on upper section of plant.
Florets
Flower hood extends well beyond the bracts.
