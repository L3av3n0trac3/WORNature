Erythranthe tilingii

Common name
Tiling's Monkeyflower
Family
Phrymaceae
Family common name
Lopseed Family
Blooms
May - October
Habitat
Seeps, wet meadows, at mid to high elevations in mountains.
Distinctions
leaves not hairy. Easily confused with M. gutattus, which has stolons, usually more obvious red dots on throat, and grows only to mid elevations. In areas wheere they come together, they hybridize.
