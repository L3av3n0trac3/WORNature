Triteleia hyacinthina

Common name
White Brodiaea
Family
Liliaceae
Family common name
Lily family
Blooms
March - August
Habitat
Spring-wet grasslands to mid-elevations
Differences compared to T. grandiflora
Flowers white, sometimes tinted purple outside. Tubular part of flower is a shallow bowl. Flower lobe mid-veins green.
