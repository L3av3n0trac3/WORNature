Castilleja arachnoidea

Common name
Cobwebby Paintbrush
Family
Orobanchaceae
Family common name
Broomrape Family
Blooms
May - September
Habitat
Open dry rocks or summits of mountains at mid and high elevations.
Inflorescence color
Dull yellow to rusty red.
Stature
3-12", erect, covered with white-wooly hairs.
Leaves
3/4 to 1-1/4 in. long, entire or divided, 1 or 2 pairs of somewhat dished lobes.
Florets
Bracts 1/4 to 3/4 inch long, deeply divided; 3-5 rounded, dull yellow to rusty red lobes. Flowers pale green or purple-red.
